Privacy Policy

How Navigating The End collects, uses, and protects personal information.

This is a legal document. Please read it carefully.

Defined terms

“Company” means From the Couch, Heidi Willow. “Website” means navigatingend.site. “Privacy Officer” means Heather Gowrie at heather@gowrie.biz. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the Company.

Collecting and using personal information

Contact information

When you use a contact form or make a purchase, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, mailing address, phone number, and email address, so we can communicate with you and fulfill purchases. If you provide social media information, it may be used to view your social media presence according to that provider’s settings.

Financial and profile information

For purchases, billing and payment information may be collected or processed by a third-party payment processor. We may create a profile from contact or purchase information to identify you and provide access to digital products.

Usage and analytics

As you navigate the Website, technical information may be collected to improve the Website and user experience. Session analytics may collect pages visited, time spent, navigation patterns, device specifications, internet connection information, and IP address. We do not collect location information, although third-party advertising technologies may infer approximate location from their own data practices.

How long we keep information

We keep contact and profile information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, to comply with law, enforce agreements, or resolve disputes. Payment information is processed by payment providers, which may retain information under their own privacy policies. You may ask us to delete collected information by contacting the Privacy Officer, subject to legal and operational exceptions.

Cookies, pixels, and tracking

Cookies are browser-stored information that can remember preferences and help recognize prior visits. Pixels or tags may tell us that something was clicked. These technologies may communicate browser, device, advertising ID, email-open, and technical information. You can instruct your browser to reject cookies and similar technologies, but some parts of a website may not function correctly. Because there is no uniform Do Not Track standard, this Website may not be able to read or respond to every signal.

Email marketing and disclosure

We aim to use email respectfully and do not sell or share email addresses for payment. Emails comply with applicable law and offer an unsubscribe option. Unsubscribing removes you from routine promotional communications, though purchase-related messages may still be sent. We disclose personal information only when necessary to provide a service or product, enforce agreements, work with service providers, or comply with law.

Security and service providers

We use reasonable, industry-standard methods and reputable third-party vendors to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Analytics may include Google Analytics. Payment processors may include Stripe and PayPal; review their privacy policies before making a payment.

California and European Union rights

California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of certain sharing or sale, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and receive non-discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights. We may need to verify identity before acting on a request.

European Union residents may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, data portability, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, subject to applicable law.

Children and other websites

We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 16. People under 18 may use the Website only with a parent or guardian’s permission. The Website may link to third-party sites; their privacy policies govern their own practices.

Contact us

For copies of personal information, privacy questions, or requests, contact the Privacy Officer: heather@gowrie.biz.
